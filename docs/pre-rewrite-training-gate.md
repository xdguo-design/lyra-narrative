# Pre-Rewrite Training Gate

## Purpose

Do not restart the novel rewrite until the Writer, Editor, Reader Gate, and long-form controls have all passed their training gates.

The current Chapter 5 exercises prove coached improvement inside one scene. They do not yet prove transfer, multi-chapter stability, or editor stability.

## Gate 1 — Writer transfer mastery

Required:
- finish the Chapter 5 integrated scene;
- pass integrated Blind Reader Gate;
- run at least two transfer exercises with different scene engines:
  1. non-investigation family/economic conflict;
  2. authority/work conflict or action/execution scene;
- all three dimensions must reach at least STABLE in one transfer and no dimension may remain NEEDS_WORK:
  - scene direction;
  - character behavior;
  - language rhythm;
- at least one dimension should reach TRANSFERABLE before production rewrite begins.

Failure means more deliberate practice, not chapter rewriting.

## Gate 2 — Reader calibration

A single Reader pass is insufficient.

Use three reader perspectives on representative scenes:
- First-pass comprehension reader: can explain what happened without rereading;
- Character/relationship reader: can explain what each person wants and what changed between them;
- Momentum reader: can state what they now expect and whether the scene generated curiosity rather than confusion.

Hard rules:
- critical semantic / causal / motivation / relationship gaps = 0;
- INTENTIONAL_UNKNOWN is allowed only when the unknown item can be named;
- AMBIGUOUS_GAP must be explicitly intentional and non-blocking;
- “later explanation will fix it” is not accepted.

## Gate 3 — Editor deliberate practice

The Aesthetic Editor currently has rules but has not completed deliberate-practice training.

Build and run:
1. editor baseline diagnosis;
2. selection exercise: identify what must be preserved, cut, compressed, or slowed;
3. voice-preservation exercise;
4. anti-overediting exercise;
5. Reader comparison: before vs after;
6. transfer edit on a different scene type.

Editor must prove:
- clarity improves without adding explanation;
- prose improves without becoming more literary by default;
- character voices remain distinguishable;
- facts, motives, clue sources, and event order remain frozen;
- a good rough edge is preserved when it belongs to the character;
- Reader Gate improves or stays equal after editing, never degrades.

## Gate 4 — Failure corpus / regression set

Freeze known failures as reusable regression cases.

Minimum corpus:
- vague unknown boundary: “县衙那边也没完”;
- generic vague object: “事情还没完”;
- unsupported cognition: “他终于明白了”;
- ambiguous relational action: “她把钱收起来。算了。”;
- repeated short-sentence cadence;
- “看了看 / 没说话 / 停了一下” used as default rhythm;
- interrogation Q&A where characters cooperate for plot;
- protagonist always makes the correct bounded inference;
- convenient clue appears exactly when needed;
- chapter-ending summary / AI moral;
- repeated chapter engine: clue -> verify -> new clue;
- editor over-polishes all characters into one voice.

Every pipeline change must continue to pass this corpus.

## Gate 5 — Character stress tests

Before rewrite, stress-test the core cast rather than only reading character cards.

For each recurring core character, run at least two pressure contexts:
- superior vs subordinate;
- family/intimate relationship;
- money/status pressure;
- accusation/evidence pressure;
- opportunity to lie or avoid;
- conflict between short-term interest and long-term relationship.

At minimum test:
- 陈安;
- 陈小满;
- 柳氏;
- 赵六;
- 周虎;
- 孙成;
- 刘三爷 before his first substantial scene.

Pass condition:
the same character remains recognizable while behavior changes with status, leverage, and relationship.

## Gate 6 — Long-form scene and pacing training

Single-scene quality is not enough.

Run a three-chapter mini-arc simulation without publishing it.

The three chapters must use different primary engines, for example:
- family/debt;
- yamen work/status;
- investigation/action.

Review across the sequence:
- no repeated opening mechanism;
- no repeated humor button;
- no repeated evidence-discovery engine;
- no repeated chapter-end hook type;
- family/economy/status/progression remain active alongside the case;
- protagonist's flaw produces consequences, not only competence gains;
- proficiency system appears only when real practice/results justify it.

Pass condition:
the three chapters feel like one novel with changing movement, not three variants of the same scene.

## Gate 7 — Genre promise matrix for this novel

Freeze the novel's chapter-level genre promises before rewrite.

Core promises:
- bottom-tier yamen survival;
- money/debt/family pressure;
- proficiency growth through real practice;
- status/career progression;
- county life and social hierarchy;
- local cases and danger.

The investigation line is one engine, not the entire genre.

For each 5–8 chapter block, verify that at least three non-investigation promises materially affect choices and outcomes.

## Gate 8 — Rewrite freeze pack

Only after training gates pass, freeze the inputs used for the actual rewrite:

- architecture / world assumptions;
- proficiency rules;
- character behavior cards;
- 30-chapter arc;
- chapter-function matrix;
- Writer Craft Profile;
- Editor Craft Profile;
- Reader Gate v3;
- failure corpus;
- story-state reset point;
- prohibited recurring patterns.

After freeze, rewrite from Chapter 1 in order. Do not patch old prose chapter by chapter.

## Rewrite readiness definition

The novel may restart only when:

- Writer: no NEEDS_WORK; scene/character/rhythm all >= STABLE in transfer;
- Reader: zero critical Reader Gap on representative tests;
- Editor: passes voice-preservation and anti-overediting transfer;
- Long-form: three-chapter mini-arc passes repetition/genre-balance review;
- Core character stress tests pass;
- failure corpus regression passes;
- freeze pack is versioned.

Until then, training artifacts are not treated as final novel prose.
